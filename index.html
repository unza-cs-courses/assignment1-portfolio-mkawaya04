About Me

My name is Mapalo Kawaya, and I am a fourth-year Software Engineering student at the University of Zambia. My interest in computer science began in secondary school because I took computer science and thought to myself "hmm this actually is interesting." I pursued my interest and now I have a strong interest in mobile application development and enjoy building digital solutions that are practical and user friendly. I focus on developing Android and iOS applications that are reliable, scalable, and designed with a good user experience in mind. I am also a passionate STEM and technology enthusiast who enjoys learning about new tools and technologies that can improve how software systems are built. Currently, I am interning at the National Institute for Scientific and Industrial Research (NISIR), where I am contributing to the development of a mobile application. This experience is helping me strengthen my skills in software development, teamwork, and problem solving in a real-world environment. Through my studies and projects, I have developed strong interests in mobile technologies, software engineering practices, and innovative digital systems. I enjoy collaborating with others, exchanging ideas, and building impactful technological solutions while continuously expanding my professional network in the software engineering and innovation space. My goal is to build softwares that will solve problems and make life easier for the world.
